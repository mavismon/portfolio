Ending the poolside towel wars

A digital concierge that turns TUI's most-complained-about friction into a premium guest experience — and a new revenue stream.

Every morning at TUI's sunniest resorts, guests race to drape towels over loungers at dawn, then disappear for hours. I built the strategy and prototype for a Digital Concierge that replaces manual towel reservations with a real-time, automated management system.

01

The friction

The “Dawn Rush”

Guests race to reserve loungers at dawn with towels — then vanish for hours. The loungers sit empty but “reserved,” the best spots vanish before breakfast, and families who can't sit together are the first to complain. Staff get caught refereeing disputes they have no tools to resolve. It's the holiday equivalent of road rage, and it shows up loud and clear in the guest data.

☀️

“Seat ghosting”: a lounger reserved free all day, but actually used for only ~3 of the 8.2 hours of sun. The culture is first-come-first-served — not active use.

Why it matters to TUI

The business context

TUI is the world's leading integrated tourism group, managing the whole journey from booking to flight, hotel, and cruise — its brand purpose is to “create the moments that make life richer.” Sun loungers sound trivial, but they sit right at the heart of the hotel experience that drives repeat bookings. A 12% complaint rate on something this visible quietly erodes the guest satisfaction TUI's growth depends on.

03

Three routes explored

Rather than jump to an app, I pressure-tested three phased options against budget, guest impact, and risk.

PHASE 1 · LOW BUDGET

The “Fair Play” Policy

A 30-minute turnover rule at peak hours — if a lounger sits empty for 30 min, staff move the towel to a holding area.
  • Zero capital investment
  • Immediate results
  • Restores fairness
  • Conflict potential
  • Labour intensive
  • Perception risk
PHASE 2 · MID BUDGET

The Digital Concierge App

A live “Lounger Map” in the TUI app — guests check in by QR and get auto-notified when their 2 free hours expire.
  • Automated fairness
  • Live availability
  • No confrontation
  • Boosts revenue
  • Admin heavy
  • Added complexity
★ CHOSEN DIRECTION
PHASE 3 · HIGH BUDGET

Premium “Family Cabana” Rentals

Shaded, bookable “Family Hubs” for a daily fee — a guaranteed home base for the guests most likely to complain.
  • Guaranteed peace of mind
  • New revenue stream
  • Frees the main deck
  • Space constraints
  • £6,250 up-front each
  • Doesn't stop ghosting
🎯

The Digital Concierge wins: it shifts the burden from staff to system, scales across resorts at low capex, and is the only option that turns the friction itself into measurable revenue. Fair Play becomes its day-one rule; cabanas become a later upsell.

05

How it works

Strategic shift: instead of a standalone app, the concierge lives inside TUI's existing infrastructure — cutting build cost and meeting guests where they already are.

1

Scan the QR

Each lounger has a QR. Scanning deep-links to the TUI app if installed, or opens the browser — no download required.

2

Universal login

Guests sign in with room number + last name, unifying every profile across the TUI ecosystem.

3

Claim & enjoy

Two free hours start. The live map updates instantly so others see what's genuinely taken.

4

Auto turnaround

A nudge before expiry lets guests renew or release; staff see vacated loungers in real time.
