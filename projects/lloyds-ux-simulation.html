Lloyds Banking Group: two UX design job simulations

Real-world UX practice with Lloyds' design team via Forage — from competitor research and surveys to a customer value proposition defended in front of stakeholders.

Role
UX Designer (simulation)
Timeline
Jun – Jul 2025
Type
Research & strategy
Tools
Surveys, data visualization

Introduction simulation — June 2025

  • Competitor research and UX enhancement for Lloyds' digital banking experience.
  • Designed and analyzed customer surveys with 50 respondents to prioritize feature improvements.
  • Ran ethnographic and observational research, producing actionable insights for navigation and security features.
  • Used data visualization to make survey findings land with non-research stakeholders.

Advanced simulation — July 2025

  • Data-driven research into customer spending habits to surface pain points and opportunities.
  • Developed a Customer Value Proposition aligning user needs with business goals.
  • Designed and delivered a stakeholder presentation communicating insights and proposed solutions.
  • Practiced data synthesis, cross-functional collaboration, and defending design decisions.
Banking UX lives or dies on trust. The biggest insight from both simulations: users don't want more features — they want fewer surprises around money.
